# hfs

This project is a test case for the Home Fab System and a source of HFS elements. Linkage runs both ways.

- **HFS informs the crown.** Check each significant decision against current HFS state: decision log, taxonomy, environment and operating-model documents, file-location rules.
- **The crown informs HFS.** Every significant decision gets an HFS note: followed, diverged, or new.

## Files

- `divergence-log.md` where we chose not to follow an HFS rule: what HFS says, what we did, why, evidence, and the proposed HFS outcome (no change, clarify, amend, new pattern).
- `extraction-candidates.md` reusable principles, templates, modules and failed assumptions waiting to be extracted into HFS.
- `open-actions.md` HFS gaps and follow-ups that need action.

Nothing is published from here directly. Validated pieces are extracted into their proper HFS home (print profiles to Shop, guide content to Knowledge, prices and terms to Business).
