## DR-001 · 2026-10-21 · Crown in its own repository

- **HFS source:** INT-INF-GDE-001 (GitHub setup, v0.3 draft) says text documents are mastered in the `hfs` repository, with Drive and Claude Project copies refreshed from it. Only part of that guide has been read for this record.
- **What we did:** the crown has its own repo, `king-nothing-crown`.
- **Why:** Brian's decision. The crown mixes code, hardware records and costume work, and was not conceived as an HFS document set.
- **Proposed reconciliation:** treat this repo as a working folder. Validated, reusable outputs are extracted into `hfs` under Knowledge, Shop or Business. This matches STD-008 section 5.3 (nothing is published from a working folder directly).
- **Evidence:** reasoned assumption.
- **Proposed HFS outcome:** clarify. The guide should say whether project repos are permitted and how extraction into `hfs` works.
- **Status:** open, awaiting Brian's review.

## DR-002 · 2026-10-21 · Fusion licence

- **HFS source:** decision log (2026-10-06) and STD-008 section 6 note that Fusion's free personal licence does not allow selling designs; use FreeCAD or a paid licence for anything sold. Marked "to confirm".
- **What we did:** Brian is modeling the crown in Fusion.
- **Why it may not diverge:** a personal costume is within personal use. It matters only if the crown CAD becomes an HFS module or product.
- **Proposed HFS outcome:** clarify. Add a rule for when a personal-use model becomes a sold artifact.
- **Status:** open. Confirm the licence terms before any reuse.
