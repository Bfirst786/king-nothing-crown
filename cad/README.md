# cad

Parameter lists, Fusion export notes and coordinate conventions. Keep the head-fit parameters (607 mm circumference at the crown track, liner allowance, tilt, maximum height) here with their evidence level.

Fusion source files, STL and 3MF files stay in Google Drive (`KING NOTHING/cad`). Link to them from here; do not commit large binaries.

Do not use the module study's dimensions (dock envelope, electronics depth) as manufacturing values. They are planning envelopes until a real TFT and controller are measured.
